## U99 Profile units mutate the profile

- AC-1 `report` accepts a mutation of `.aidlc/profile.json` even when the unit's diff changes it.
## U100 Research holds on decisions
From the second project (2026-10-25): a research hold on decision 0003 (which MTA core) was recorded, but `aidlc research 0003` found no question and `next` skipped it ([State](../design/state.md)).
- AC-1 A decision held with `research:` gets a brief from `aidlc research <number>`, and `next` asks for its researcher until a digest answers it.
## U101 Decisions before units
From the second project (2026-10-25): with I-1 approved and its architecture decisions still drafts, `next` and the Stop hook demanded units ([State](../design/state.md#next)).
- AC-1 An approved intent without units that links draft decisions: `next` asks to finish those with open points, then for their approval, before units.
## U102 Research holds on any doc
From the second project (2026-10-25): research holds on design docs (a capability catalogue) were recorded but not found by `research` or `next` ([State](../design/state.md#next)).
- AC-1 A research hold on any doc in the bundle gets a brief by the doc's name and holds `next` until answered.
## U103 Research digests fit
From the second project (2026-10-25): nearly every digest came back over the 4,000 cap, with ~1,800 chars of question and sources before any finding; one had an unquoted colon in its description ([OKF validation](../design/okf-validation.md)).
- AC-1 A Research doc is capped at 6,000 chars; the brief's frontmatter quotes title and description.
## U104 Re-approval with new drafts

- AC-1 A re-approval names the draft requirements the stale docs cite, and is never delegated while it holds one.
