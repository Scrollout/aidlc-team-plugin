## U100 Research holds on decisions

- AC-1 A decision held with `research:` gets a brief from `aidlc research <number>`, and `next` asks for its researcher until a digest answers it.

## U102 Research holds on any doc

## U103 Research digests fit

- AC-1 A Research doc is capped at 6,000 chars; the brief's frontmatter quotes title and description.

## U109 Gate runs fresh; fix rounds after a refusal
From both projects (2026-10-25): Go's test cache replayed a pass over a mutation, failing a sound proof; after a gate refusal `next` offered the merge again while the fix builder ran; the research brief named the plugin's cap, not the project's, and no actor format ([Gate](../design/gate.md)).

- AC-2 A unit the gate refused whose builder was launched after the refusal is building.

## U110 Stamp headroom at check time
From the second project (2026-10-25): `approve` refused decision 0012 twice, its stamps taking it over the cap by 17 and then 1 char, a round each ([Approvals](../design/approvals.md)).
- AC-1 `check` warns `HOUSE-SIZE` on a draft or stale approvable doc its next approval stamps would take over its cap, naming the chars to trim; `approve` counts with the same function.
## U111 Findings name their level
From the first migration (2026-10-25): a finding line gave no level; a builder took PROFILE-ARCH's from the code table (error) while `check` counted it a warning in the adopted project ([CLI](../design/cli.md)).
- AC-1 Each finding line ends with its run-time level; `upgrade` still matches findings by place and code.
## U112 Card size before approval
From the second project (2026-10-25): `card U-14` refused at 6,068 chars of 6,000; the card renders only an approved doc, so each trim cost an owner approval, three rounds ([Unit card](../design/unit-card.md)).
- AC-1 `check` warns `PLAN-CARD` on a draft or stale live unit whose card, rendered from the doc on disk, is over the cap, with the size `card` counts.
## U113 Approve all or nothing; one stamp per approver
From the first migration (2026-10-25): `approve 0001 0001 …`, as `next` printed it for two decisions numbered 0001, stamped one file twice and refused it over the cap after writing; each re-approval added a stamp, so a doc near its cap needed a trim, itself an edit, before every round ([Approvals](../design/approvals.md)).
- AC-1 `approve` writes nothing when any doc it names is refused, and stamps a doc named twice once.
- AC-2 A re-approval replaces its approver's earlier stamp and keeps the others'.
- AC-3 `next` names a decision by its file name when another decision shares its number.
## U114 Quick lane runs the first level; a red base at the gate
From the first migration (2026-10-25): a quick unit split a doc a script test read and merged running no level; the next unit's merge then failed `GATE-RUN` on the red base, and `next` kept offering it ([Gate](../design/gate.md), [Base CI](../design/base-ci.md)).
- AC-1 A quick-lane merge runs the first level in full; a failure refuses it.
- AC-2 A level that fails on the base alone too is `GATE-BASE`: the base is red until a merge, only fix units build or merge, and without a live one `next` asks for it.
## U115 Reviews in parallel; builders beside the CI wait
The owner (2026-10-25): more speed on both projects. A unit's focus reviews ran one after another, and each merge held new builders for the base's CI, 7 to 14 minutes a link in a chain of units ([State](../design/state.md#next), [Base CI](../design/base-ci.md)).
- AC-1 `next` names every required focus without a pass for the current diff; the skill starts one reviewer per focus in one message.
- AC-2 With `ci.wait`, a pending or unknown CI on a pushed base no longer holds builders: `next` builds and asks for `aidlc ci wait` in the background.

## U118 Slices stay vertical
The owner (2026-10-25), high priority: prevent and fix the drift of both projects from vertical end-to-end slices. The second project cut its intent as layers with the walking skeleton behind them; the first project's portal and identity slices waited behind dependency and renaming fix units, chained only because `PLAN-SCOPE` demanded a `depends` for any shared file ([Grid](../design/grid.md#slices), [Plan lint](../design/plan-lint.md)).
- AC-1 `PLAN-SLICE` reports an intent with more live units than `plan.sliceUnits` without `# Slices`, a live unit on no slice line, a slice whose units have no top-level AC, a slice wider than `plan.sliceUnits` (default 3), and a unit waiting on a later slice.
- AC-2 `PLAN-SLICE` reports a unit of an intent waiting on a fix unit not yet merged.
- AC-3 `PLAN-SCOPE` asks for `depends` only between overlapping units of one intent; `next` never starts a unit whose scope overlaps one started and not merged, and puts intent units before fix units at the same priority.
## U119 The card cap counts lines
The owner (2026-10-25): budget the cards to 100 lines instead of chars. The second project's U-2 passed `check` while `card` refused it: PLAN-CARD sized only unapproved units, and a char cap pushed the lead to squeeze wording ([Unit card](../design/unit-card.md)).
- AC-1 The card cap is 100 lines; `card`, PLAN-CARD and the budget test count lines.
- AC-2 PLAN-CARD sizes every unit not yet merged, approved ones too.
## U120 A pipe after cd keeps the folder
From the second project (2026-10-25): `cd ~/platform-infra && git push -q origin main 2>&1 | tail -3` was refused as a raw push of the base, because any pipe or `||` in the command voided every `cd` ([Hooks](../design/hooks.md#wiring)).
- AC-1 A `cd` is unresolved only in a command with a subshell or when the `cd` itself sits in a pipeline or background job; a pipe or `||` after it keeps its folder.
