A project started from scratch runs its inception the way the method intends: research on any doc, architecture decisions before units, and approvals that name every draft they need.

## U99 Profile units mutate the profile
A unit whose AC is a profile setting counted as tests-only, and its only valid mutation lay in the vendored CLI ([Back-fill](../design/backfill.md)).
- AC-1 `report` accepts a mutation of `.aidlc/profile.json` even when the unit's diff changes it.
## U100 Research holds on decisions
A research hold on a decision was recorded, but `aidlc research <number>` found no question and `next` skipped it ([State](../design/state.md)).
- AC-1 A decision held with `research:` gets a brief from `aidlc research <number>`, and `next` asks for its researcher until a digest answers it.
## U101 Decisions before units
With an intent approved and its architecture decisions still drafts, `next` and the Stop hook demanded units ([State](../design/state.md#next)).
- AC-1 An approved intent without units that links draft decisions: `next` asks to finish those with open points, then for their approval, before units.
## U102 Research holds on any doc
A research hold on a design doc was recorded but found by neither `research` nor `next` ([State](../design/state.md#next)).
- AC-1 A research hold on any doc in the docs bundle gets a brief by the doc's name and holds `next` until answered.
## U103 Research digests fit
A digest carries about 1,800 chars of question and sources before any finding, so most exceed a 4,000 cap; an unquoted colon in a description breaks its front matter ([OKF validation](../design/okf-validation.md)).
- AC-1 A Research doc is capped at 6,000 chars; the brief's frontmatter quotes title and description.
## U104 Re-approval with new drafts
`next` asked to re-approve the stale docs but left out the new draft requirements they cite ([State](../design/state.md#next)).
- AC-1 A re-approval names the draft requirements the stale docs cite, and is never delegated while it holds one.

## U105 Slice architecture by default
A codebase keeps its shape over its life only when each module owns its data; the method makes slice architecture with data ownership the default ([Slice architecture](../design/architecture-model.md)). The CLI budget goes to 4,000 lines.
- AC-1 With modules, `check` fails `PROFILE-ARCH` without data ownership (a warning in an adopted project) and warns without `acyclic` or a module's `api`.
- AC-2 `init` writes constitution rule 9, data ownership.
## U106 Edited decisions go back to the owner
An approved decision edited afterwards got no re-approval from `next`; the template constitution plus a project's rules reach a 4,000 cap at once ([State](../design/state.md#next), [OKF validation](../design/okf-validation.md)).
- AC-1 An approved decision edited since its approval is stale: `next` asks the owner to re-approve it.
- AC-2 A Principles doc is capped at 6,000 chars.
## U107 Walking skeleton first
A plan built domain by domain reaches end to end only at its last units, so the target is proven last ([Plan lint](../design/plan-lint.md)).
- AC-1 Plan lint warns `PLAN-SKELETON` when an intent's first top-level AC depends on more than half of its product units; `next` asks for a walking skeleton first.
## U108 Identities, not secrets
Runtimes, services and data authenticate to each other by workload identity, never by a stored secret, as a permanent rule of the method ([Workload identity](../design/workload-identity.md)).
- AC-1 The gate fails `GATE-IDENTITY` on an added line outside the test paths with database trust auth, a URL holding a password or a client secret value.
- AC-2 `init` writes constitution rule 10.
## U109 Gate runs fresh; fix rounds after a refusal
Go's test cache can replay a pass over a mutation, failing a sound proof; after a gate refusal `next` offered the merge again while the fix builder ran; the research brief named the plugin's cap, not the project's, and no actor format ([Gate](../design/gate.md)).
- AC-1 Gate level runs set `gate.fresh` (GOFLAGS=-count=1), added to any value already set.
- AC-2 A unit the gate refused whose builder was launched after the refusal is building.
- AC-3 The research brief states the project's Research cap and `claude-code/<model id>` as the actor.
## U110 Stamp headroom at check time
`approve` can refuse a doc whose own stamps take it over its cap, and each trim costs a round ([Approvals](../design/approvals.md)).
- AC-1 `check` warns `HOUSE-SIZE` on a draft or stale approvable doc its next approval stamps would take over its cap, naming the chars to trim; `approve` counts with the same function.
## U111 Findings name their level
A finding line gave no level, so a reader took it from the code table (error) where `check` counted a warning in an adopted project ([CLI](../design/cli.md)).
- AC-1 Each finding line ends with its run-time level; `upgrade` still matches findings by place and code.
## U112 Card size before approval
`card` refuses a card over its cap, and renders only an approved doc, so each trim costs an owner approval ([Unit card](../design/bolt-card.md)).
- AC-1 `check` warns `PLAN-CARD` on a draft or stale live unit whose card, rendered from the doc on disk, is over the cap, with the size `card` counts.
## U113 Approve all or nothing; one stamp per approver
With two decisions sharing a number, `next` printed the number twice, and `approve` stamped one file twice and refused it over the cap after writing; each re-approval added a stamp, so a doc near its cap needed a trim, itself an edit, before every round ([Approvals](../design/approvals.md)).
- AC-1 `approve` writes nothing when any doc it names is refused, and stamps a doc named twice once.
- AC-2 A re-approval replaces its approver's earlier stamp and keeps the others'.
- AC-3 `next` names a decision by its file name when another decision shares its number.
## U114 Quick lane runs the first level; a red base at the gate
A quick unit could merge running no level and break the base; the next unit's merge then failed `GATE-RUN` on the red base, and `next` kept offering it ([Gate](../design/gate.md), [Base CI](../design/base-ci.md)).
- AC-1 A quick-lane merge runs the first level in full; a failure refuses it.
- AC-2 A level that fails on the base alone too is `GATE-BASE`: the base is red until a merge, only fix units build or merge, and without a live one `next` asks for it.
## U115 Reviews in parallel; builders beside the CI wait
A unit's focus reviews ran one after another, and each merge held new builders for the base's CI, minutes per link in a chain of units ([State](../design/state.md#next), [Base CI](../design/base-ci.md)).
- AC-1 `next` names every required focus without a pass for the current diff; the skill starts one reviewer per focus in one message.
- AC-2 With `ci.wait`, a pending or unknown CI on a pushed base no longer holds builders: `next` builds and asks for `aidlc ci wait` in the background.
## U116 The card cap counts the unit
PLAN-CARD counted the profile's Names, Dependencies, Platform and Data sections, more than half of a card's cap, against the unit ([Unit card](../design/bolt-card.md)).
- AC-1 The card cap leaves out the Data, Names, Dependencies and Platform sections; `card` and PLAN-CARD count alike.
