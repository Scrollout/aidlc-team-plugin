# Goal
A vertical slice is planned where its story is written and checked where its code is merged, with one grammar for both ([Vertical slices checked](../design/vertical-slices.md#planned-before-built), [0024](../decisions/0024-vertical-slices-are-features-horizontal-layers.md)).

# Bolts
## B247 Slices in models
- AC-1 A model's domains and its extension list their slices as `{ kind, verb, useCase }`; `MODEL-FORMAT` reports a slice whose kind is not Command or Query, whose verb is not in the naming vocabulary, with no use case, or with a kind and verb twice in one domain.
- AC-2 `models apply` writes each model domain's doc the bundle lacks, its `# Surface` holding one line per slice with the name the registry composes and its use case, and prints the increments slice by slice, the walking skeleton's first slice through every layer first.
- AC-3 `models gaps` lists, for each model domain a module plays, the model's slices its domain doc does not list, as increments.
- AC-4 The example control-plane model lists its slices, and its Go reference names them as the registry composes them.
## B248 Slices in Units of work and intake
- AC-1 Method 12: each Unit names the slices it delivers under `Slices`, one `<Kind> <slice> [US<g>.<s>]` each; `UNIT-WORK` reports a story of the Unit no slice serves, a slice citing no story of the Unit, a kind other than Command or Query, and, with a naming registry, a slice name the registry composes from none of its verbs and the domains of the Unit's modules.
- AC-2 Intake lists each fitting model's slices beside it, and the product analyst's persona and the intent template carry the Slices part.
