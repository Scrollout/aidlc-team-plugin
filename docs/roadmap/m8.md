## U61 Mutations that break the main behaviour
- AC-1 Each mutation in a back-fill report names, in `breaks`, the behaviour of its AC that it breaks; `aidlc report` refuses one without it, and the card shows it beside the mutation.
- AC-2 The card asks for the mutation that breaks the AC's main behaviour, not only a boundary or an edge case, and the reviewer prompt says to send back one that breaks only an edge of what its AC names.

# Why
In the [M7 back-fill pilot](../pilot/m7-report.md) one of three mutations flipped a boundary only (`e.After(now)` to `!e.Before(now)`): a real break, but a narrow one, and both reviewers accepted it.
