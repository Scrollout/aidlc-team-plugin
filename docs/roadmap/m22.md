# Goal
A person who is not an engineer describes what they want, and the plan that comes out covers every sentence, says where every claim came from, and was written with a product analyst's and an architect's method ([Intake](../design/intake.md), [0019](../decisions/0019-the-lead-works-in-role-personas.md)).

# Bolts
## B213 Role personas
- AC-1 `plugin/personas/` holds a product analyst and an architect, each within 3 KB with its knowledge files. `next` names the role for each elaborate step, and the overview's non-goal narrows to simulated multi-agent meetings.
## B214 The request
- AC-1 `aidlc intake` writes `requests/Q-<n>` word for word, with sentence ids, and an edit to it is `REQUEST-AMEND`.
- AC-2 It proposes the pathway, stack and lane from the repo and the request's risk signals; the owner confirms.
## B215 Sources and coverage
- AC-1 An intent goal line, requirement statement or story without a source is `CLAIM-SOURCE`; a request sentence nothing cites is `REQUEST-COVERAGE`. Both block approval.
## B216 Corners and answers
- AC-1 The corner checklist is data; an unsettled surface is `ELAB-CORNER`.
- AC-2 A vague answer is `ELAB-VAGUE`; "not decided" stays an open question; the owner's read-back confirmation is recorded.
## B217 Personas and stories
- AC-1 A request naming two or more kinds of user needs `# Personas` and `# Stories`, each story citing requirements.
## B218 Advisory review before approval
- AC-1 `next` names `review I-<n>` (focus `intent`), and approval refuses without a review for the docs' current hash (`APPROVE-REVIEW`).
## B219 Agents get exactly their brief
- AC-1 A hook renders the card or brief as the subagent's prompt. Findings keep their ids across rounds. A subagent cannot run the lead's commands. Each agent carries its effort.
