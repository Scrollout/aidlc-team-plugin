# Goal
A person who is not an engineer describes what they want, and the plan that comes out covers every sentence, says where every claim came from, and was written with a product analyst's and an architect's method ([Intake](../design/intake.md), [0019](../decisions/0019-the-lead-works-in-role-personas.md)).

# Bolts
## B213 Role personas
- AC-1 `plugin/cli/personas/` holds a product analyst and an architect, each within 3 KB with its knowledge files. `next` names the role for each elaborate step, and the overview's non-goal narrows to simulated multi-agent meetings.
## B214 The request
- AC-1 `aidlc intake` writes `requests/Q-<n>` word for word, with sentence ids, and a hand edit to it is `DOC-GENERATED`.
- AC-2 It proposes the pathway, stack and lane from bands: verification and risk words it measures, and ambiguity, risk and assumptions the lead scores, each citing sentences. The owner confirms, and the intent records `request`, `pathway` and `lane`.
- AC-3 An intent's `lane` is a floor for its bolts.
## B215 Sources and coverage
- AC-1 A method 6 intent names its requests. Its goal lines, its stories and its draft requirements' statements cite a source that resolves: a request sentence, an answered question, a listed assumption or a fact. A missing or unresolved one is `CLAIM-SOURCE`.
- AC-2 A request sentence that neither the intent nor its requirements cite is `REQUEST-COVERAGE`. Both block approval.
## B216 Corners and answers
- AC-1 The corner checklist is data; a surface a method 7 intent leaves out or unsettled is `ELAB-CORNER`.
- AC-2 A vague answer is `ELAB-VAGUE`; "not decided" stays open (`ELAB-OPEN`); a read-back the owner has not confirmed as *looks correct* is `ELAB-OPEN`.
## B217 Personas and stories
- AC-1 A request naming two or more kinds of user (its role words) needs two or more `# Personas`, unless the intent says `onePersona`; else `ELAB-PERSONA`.
- AC-2 Each persona has a story `As a <persona>, I want…, so that…` citing one of the intent's requirements; else `ELAB-STORY`. Both block approval.
## B218 Advisory review before approval
- AC-1 For a method 9 intent, `next` names `review I-<n>`, which prints the reviewer's brief. `review record I-<n> -` with focus `intent` keeps the reviewed docs' hash, and the approval refuses without a review for the docs' current hash (`APPROVE-REVIEW`). The findings are listed for the owner and block nothing.
## B219 Agents get exactly their brief
- AC-1 The `Agent` hook replaces the lead's prompt with the card or brief the CLI renders, and sets the role's effort from `efforts`.
- AC-2 Review findings keep their ids across rounds: a fix or escalate round must answer each open one, else it is refused; a pass answers them all.
- AC-3 A subagent running an `aidlc` command outside its role's list is `GUARD-SUBAGENT`.
